Uniburst Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · App: Uniburst (package in.dsakshay.uniburst) · Developer: Uniburst (independent developer)

This policy explains what information the Uniburst mobile game ("the game", "we") and the services it uses handle when you play. We built the game to collect as little as possible: there is no account, no sign-in, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (scores, coins, levels, unlocked items, settings, missions and trophies) is saved on your phone only. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

2. Information handled by third-party services

The game uses the following services. They receive some information directly from your device under their own privacy policies.

3. Ads and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads may be personalised, using Google's consent form.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads or VIP stops the forced ads between games. Optional reward ads remain available if you choose to watch them.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

All payments are processed by Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

Data sent to the services above is encrypted in transit. Each service keeps data for as long as described in its own privacy policy. We do not sell personal information.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete your game progress by uninstalling the game. To request deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat, or for any other privacy request (access, correction or deletion, including under the GDPR or India's DPDP Act), email us at the address below. Because the game has no accounts, please include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is shown in your Google Play purchase receipt email, or in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history) so we can find your records. We reply within 30 days. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in the Play Store first.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above.
